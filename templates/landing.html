How a search works

Four steps. The AI is only in the third.

1

You set the search

Choose an area, a radius and how many results you want. Add keywords for the map and rules every result must follow.

2

Google Maps finds places

Names, addresses and coordinates come straight from Google, so none of it is made up. Closed places and anything outside your radius are dropped.

3AI step

Hermes researches them

The agent reads each place's website, checks your rules and scores it from 0 to 100, with a reason and a source link. It works in batches until enough places qualify.

4

You get a ranked list and a map

Results and pins share the same numbers. Each one links back to Google Maps, and you can export the list as CSV or JSON.

Four kinds of leads

Search for one kind at a time. Each has its own idea of a good fit and its own scoring.

Investors

Venture funds, seed funds and angel networks with a real office in your area.

Individual angels are not included, because they have no Google Maps listing to verify.

Clients

Privately held, mid-market companies in regulated or data-sensitive industries, the kind that could buy on-premise AI.

Public-sector bodies and the local offices of global giants are left out.

Workspaces

Coworking and serviced offices that could host a seed-stage AI team and receive enterprise visitors.

Partners

Technology and channel partners: NVIDIA Jetson resellers, systems integrators, edge-hardware vendors and complementary AI infrastructure firms.

Built to be checked

You shouldn't have to take a list on trust.

Is the address real?

Yes. It comes from Google Maps, and every result has a link to open the same place there and see it yourself.

Will it stay where I asked?

Yes. The radius is enforced by a distance calculation in code, not by asking the AI nicely. A place outside it is dropped.

Will it pad the list to reach my number?

No. If fewer places qualify than you asked for, you get fewer, with a note on how many were found, researched and rejected. Excluded places are listed with the reason.

Can I use the results elsewhere?

Yes. Download a CSV that opens cleanly in Excel, or copy the JSON. Phone numbers are included when Google lists one.

What it does not do

No individual angels. Only investors with an office on Google Maps can be verified, so people without a listing are not included.

Scores are estimates. They rate fit using public information. They do not predict that anyone will invest or buy.

Your rules are checked by the AI. That makes them strong, but not guaranteed the way the radius is.

Searches take a few minutes. The research step works through the places in batches.
